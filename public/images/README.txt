LANDING PORTRAIT
================

The big portrait on the landing page is loaded from:

    public/images/nusrat-portrait.jpg

To use your own exact photo (recommended), replace this file with
your image, keeping the SAME filename:

    nusrat-portrait.jpg

Tips:
- Portrait orientation works best (ideal ~900 x 1200 px, 3:4).
- JPG or PNG both work; the frame auto-crops to fit.
- No code changes required.

No-git option (auto-deploys on Vercel):
1. Open https://github.com/DEV08-cybr/Mehfil/upload/main/public/images
2. Drag your photo (named nusrat-portrait.jpg) onto the page
3. Click "Commit changes" — Vercel redeploys with your photo in ~1 minute.
